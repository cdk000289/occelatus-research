Rules we work by on your clients' profiles

The fastest way for an agency to lose a client is a suspended profile, and most suspensions start with shortcuts (a 2026 agency write-up walks through the usual ones). So here is what we commit to, in writing:

  • The business name stays the real-world name on the sign. No added keywords or city names, per Google's guidelines.
  • No fake reviews, no review gating, no discounts or gifts for reviews.
  • No virtual-office or made-up addresses, and no listings for locations that don't exist.
  • We ask you before changing the business name, address or primary category, because those edits can trigger re-verification.
  • Anything Google flags or changes on its own goes in that week's summary. A suspension notice goes to you as soon as we see it.
  • We stay a Manager, never the owner. Your client can remove us in one click.
