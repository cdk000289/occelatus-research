Common questions

Google doesn't charge for reinstatement. Why pay you?

Correct: Google's appeal process is free and you can do it yourself. Our guides show how. The Profile Fix is for owners who would rather have someone find the likely cause, fix the settings within our control and prepare the request and documents properly.

Do you guarantee my profile will be reinstated?

No. Google makes the final call, and anyone who guarantees a suspension outcome is promising something they don't control. We tell you upfront if we think something can't be fixed, and you only pay if you choose to go ahead after seeing our findings.

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add us as a Manager on the profile, which you can remove at any time.

Do I pay before you look?

No. The form is free, and we email what we found and a fix plan, usually within one business day. Payment comes only if you want us to go ahead.

What happens to the $149 if I want monthly management?
