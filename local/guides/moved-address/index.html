Moved your business? How to change your Google Business Profile address without losing it.

A move is one of the few edits Google treats like a new listing: it can ask you to verify again, and some owners report a suspension right after the change. Here is how to move the profile you already have.

Updated November 13, 2026 · Plain-English guide for business owners

Short version: Edit the address on your existing profile (don't create a new one), have proof of the new location ready, and expect to verify again. If the profile gets suspended after the move, request reinstatement with that proof instead of starting over.

1. Get your proof ready before you edit

Google reviews address changes before they go live, and third-party guides that quote Google's "Edit your Business Profile" help page note that a business that moves after verification must verify again (for example Vendasta, FirstByte). Have these to hand at the new address:

  • permanent signage with the business name, visible from outside;
  • a lease, utility bill or business licence showing the new address;
  • the ability to film a short verification video there: the street or nearby landmarks, the entrance, and you opening the door or showing tools of the trade.

2. Edit the address on the existing profile

Sign in with the account that manages the profile, open Edit profile, then Location, update the business location and check the map pin, then save (Google's steps). Change only the address in that session. Changing the name, category and address together makes the edit look like a different business.

If you visit customers and don't serve them at the new address, you may not need a public address at all. See our service-area business guide.

3. Keep your reviews

Moving the existing profile keeps its reviews with it. Google's "Move your reviews across Business Profiles" page covers relocated businesses and says that if you accidentally created and verified a new profile because of a move, you can contact Google to ask for reviews to be transferred.

4. If it gets suspended after the move

Owners regularly report this in Google's own community (example thread) and on Reddit; in some cases a video verification restored the profile. Request reinstatement from the profile and include your proof of the new address. Our suspended profile guide walks through the appeal. Google decides, and timing varies.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Creating a second profile at the new address. It leaves a duplicate and splits your reviews. If that already happened, see duplicate listings.
  • Marking the old location "permanently closed" and starting fresh, when you could move the profile you have.
  • Editing before the new place is ready. No sign, no proof, nothing to film means a failed verification.
  • Forgetting the rest of the web. Update the address on your website and directory listings too, so they match.
Avoid: anyone who guarantees reinstatement or a ranking after a move. Only Google controls that.
