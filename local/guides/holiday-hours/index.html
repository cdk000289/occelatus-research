Guide · Holiday hours

Holiday hours on Google: set them right before Thanksgiving and Christmas.

A customer drives over and finds you closed. Or your listing says "hours might differ" on the one day you're open late. Both come from the same gap: no special hours for that date. Here is how to set them in a few minutes, the common mistake that quietly closes every Thursday, and what to do when Google won't save your change.

Updated November 19, 2026 · Plain-English guide for business owners

Short version: Sign in at business.google.com, open Edit profile › Hours › Special hours, and add each holiday date: closed, shorter hours, or your normal hours. Don't change your regular weekly hours for a holiday. Confirm dates where you're open as usual too; Google suggests it so customers can trust the hours shown. Then make your website and Facebook page say the same thing.

Why it matters

Google says that on the official holidays it lists for your country, a warning may appear on your profile telling people your hours might differ, and that you can set special hours in advance for those dates. If you haven't set anything, customers see your regular hours or that warning. Either way some of them guess wrong: they come when you're closed, or they skip you when you're open.

Step 1: Add special hours for each date

Sign in at business.google.com (or search your business name on Google while signed in) and choose Edit profile. Under Hours, find Special hours and add each date. For each one choose closed, or enter the hours you'll keep that day. Google's help page says you can find saved special hours in the "Hours" tab or in "Edit profile" under "Special hours" (Google: how to set special hours).

  • Open as normal on a holiday? Add the date anyway with your usual hours. Google calls this "a good idea" because it makes clear your hours are accurate.
  • Do the whole season at once. Thanksgiving, the day after, Christmas Eve, Christmas, New Year's Eve and New Year's Day are the usual US ones. Check Google's list for your country.
  • Late-night or overnight hours? One set of special hours can't run longer than 24 hours, so split an overnight shift into two sets, one ending at midnight and one starting at midnight.

Step 2: Avoid the mistake that closes every Thursday

If you change your regular Thursday hours to "Closed" for Thanksgiving, every Thursday after it shows closed too, until someone remembers to change it back. Special hours exist for one-off dates. Use them, and leave your weekly hours alone.

Check this setting: Google says special hours only show if your business hours are set to "Open with main hours". If your profile is set to "Open with no main hours", your holiday hours won't appear. Special hours also cover up to 6 days in a row; for a longer shutdown, use the "Temporarily closed" status and switch it back when you reopen (see marked closed by mistake if it doesn't come back).

Step 3: If Google won't save them

Owners report two problems every holiday season: the change is "denied" or not approved, or the save button just spins. What to try:

  • Set them early. Google says edits usually take up to 10 minutes to review but can take longer, so don't leave it to the day before.
  • Change one thing at a time. Saving hours together with a name, category or address change makes extra review more likely.
  • Make your website agree. Put the same holiday hours on your website and Facebook page. Google checks public info and may not approve a change it can't confirm.
  • Try another route. If the editor in Search won't save, try the Google Maps app (your profile › Edit profile), or the other way round.
  • Still stuck? If an edit is rejected, Google's help explains how you might be able to appeal.

Step 4: Check that it worked

A day later, search your business name on Google and open your listing on a phone. Tap the hours: the holiday date should show the hours you set. If something else shows, Google may have taken a suggested edit; our guide on when Google changes your info covers the 4-day window to reject it. Other apps such as Apple Maps or Yelp keep their own hours, so update those separately.
