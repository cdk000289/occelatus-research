Lost access to your Google Business Profile? How to get it back.

A marketing agency set it up and never handed it over. An employee who left still controls it. You bought the business and the old owner kept the profile. These are some of the most common Business Profile problems. Here is the route Google provides, what to do when nobody answers, and how to make sure it never happens again.

Updated November 17, 2026 · Plain-English guide for business owners

Short version: Go to business.google.com/add, find your business and choose Request access. The current owner gets an email and has 3 days to respond. If they don't, you may get the option to claim and verify it yourself. Service-area businesses (no shop or office) contact Google support and ask to "Transfer ownership of listing". Once you're back in, make sure you are the owner and anyone helping you is only a manager.

First: check you aren't already in

Google's own tip: if you search for your business and can't see a "Claim this business" or "Own this business" button, you might already be the owner, just under a different Google account. Try the accounts you, your office manager or your web person used, including old Gmail addresses. Also check whether you're a manager rather than an owner: managers can edit most things but can't remove other users.

Step 1: Request ownership

From Google's "Request ownership of a Business Profile" page:

  • Go to business.google.com/add and enter your business name and address.
  • Pick your business. You'll see "Someone else may manage this Business Profile."
  • Choose Request access, fill in the form and submit.

Google emails you a confirmation and notifies the current owner, who has 3 days to respond. Keep that confirmation email: it's how you continue the process.

Service-area business (you go to customers and have no shop or office)? Google says to contact support instead and choose "Transfer ownership of listing" as the issue.

Step 2: If they approve, deny or ignore you

  • Approved: you get an email and can manage the profile.
  • Ignored for 3 days: open the confirmation email, choose View request → Verify and follow the steps. Google notes this option "isn't always available".
  • Denied: you can still suggest edits to the listing and Google says you can try to appeal.

When verifying, use details that match the real business: the address on your signs and paperwork, the business phone, and a website or registration that shows the same name. A mismatch is the most common reason verification stalls (see our verification failed guide).

If an agency is holding it

Google's third-party policies for agencies are clear: "all end customers must retain ownership or co-ownership of their Business Profile at all times," and the business decides whether a partner gets owner, manager or no access. Ask the agency in writing to make you the primary owner. If they won't, request ownership as above, and Google's policy page also links a form to report a third party that breaks these rules.

If an ex-employee or the previous owner has it

Same route: request ownership, then claim after 3 days if offered. If Google wants to verify by postcard to an address that's wrong on the listing, fix the address through Suggest an edit first, or ask support for another method and have proof ready: a business licence or registration, a utility bill and photos of your signage. Google's ownership guidelines also cover what happens when a business changes hands.

Avoid: creating a second profile for the same business to get around it. That makes a duplicate, which can get the new one suspended (see duplicate listings). Never pay anyone who asks for your Google password or promises guaranteed recovery.

Stop it happening again

  • Make a Google account owned by the business (not a person) the primary owner.
  • Add agencies, staff and freelancers as managers, not owners. You can remove a manager at any time.
  • Remove people when they leave, the same week.

That's how we work too: you stay the owner and add us as a manager, which you can undo whenever you like.
