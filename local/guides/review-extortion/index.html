Bad reviews and a demand to pay? How to report Google review extortion.

A handful of 1- and 2-star reviews land in a day or two from people you've never served. Then a message arrives on WhatsApp, Telegram or email: pay, and the reviews go away. Here's what Google says to do, the evidence to collect, and how to handle the reviews while you wait.

Updated November 26, 2026 · Plain-English guide for business owners

Short version: Don't pay and don't negotiate. Screenshot every demand, collect the link to each fake review, and file Google's merchant extortion report form. Reply calmly to each review and report each one too. Google investigates and tells you the result; nobody can promise when or whether the reviews come down.

How the scam usually looks

  • A sudden cluster of 1- and 2-star reviews, often with little or no text, from accounts with no history of being your customer.
  • Then a demand: money, gift cards, crypto, free goods or services, in exchange for removing them. Sometimes the threat is "more reviews unless you pay".
  • Google describes exactly this pattern on its help page and calls it a serious policy violation (Google: report negative review extortion scams). Search Engine Land covered the new report form when it launched (Search Engine Land, Nov 2025).
Don't pay, don't bargain, don't offer a discount to make it stop. Google says engaging or paying can encourage more attempts and doesn't guarantee the reviews are removed.

Collect the evidence first (10 minutes)

  1. Screenshot every message from the person making the demand: WhatsApp, Telegram, text, email, social media. Make sure the date, time and their number, address or profile are visible.
  2. Copy the link to each suspicious review. On your profile, open the review and use Share to copy its link (Google: manage reviews).
  3. Write down two times: when you first noticed the burst of bad reviews, and when the first demand arrived.
  4. Note any names, usernames, phone numbers or emails tied to the demands, and any earlier attempts.

File Google's extortion report

  1. Open the help page "Report negative review extortion scams on your Business Profile" and follow its link to the merchant extortion report form.
  2. Fill in your contact details, your business name, address and profile link, details of the reviews, and how they contacted you.
  3. Attach the screenshots and paste the review links. Submit.
  4. Google investigates and notifies you of the result. It won't share the details of its investigation.

This form is only for cases where someone has actually demanded money or favours. For bad reviews without a demand (spam, off-topic, a competitor, someone who was never a customer), report each review for its policy reason instead; our bad review guide covers that, and Google explains it here.

While you wait

  • Reply to each review, briefly and calmly. Something like: "We have no record of serving you and have reported this review to Google." Future customers read replies; keep it factual and don't mention the scammer's demands in public.
  • Report each review with the reason that fits (for example spam or conflict of interest), once each.
  • Keep asking every recent customer for a review, the same way you always do. Ask all of them, not only the happy ones; Google doesn't allow picking who to ask.
  • If you're threatened or there's a lot of money at stake, you can also report it to the police or the FTC at ReportFraud.ftc.gov.

Common questions

Should I pay to get the fake reviews removed?

No. Google says not to engage with or pay the people demanding money, and not to try to settle it yourself by offering money or services. Paying can invite more demands and doesn't guarantee the reviews come down.

How do I report review extortion to Google?

Use Google's merchant extortion report form, linked from the Business Profile help page 'Report negative review extortion scams'. Attach screenshots of the demands showing date, time and sender, links to each suspicious review, any names, numbers or profiles of the people involved, and when the reviews and the demand started.

How long until the reviews are removed?

Nobody can promise a removal or a date. Google investigates each report and notifies you of the result, but it doesn't share details of the investigation. Meanwhile you can still reply calmly to each review and report each one for its policy violation.
