Google shows your business as closed when it's open? How to reopen your profile.

A "Permanently closed" or "Temporarily closed" label on Maps and Search can quietly send customers elsewhere. Here is how to check the status and put it right.

Updated November 12, 2026 · Plain-English guide for business owners

Short version: Sign in to the profile, check the business status, and mark the location open with its main hours. If you can't, or it keeps reverting, ask Business Profile support. Don't create a second profile.

1. Check what Google actually shows

Search your business name on Google and Maps in a private window. Note whether it says "Permanently closed" or "Temporarily closed", and whether the address and hours are right.

2. Reopen it if you manage the profile

Google's help pages "Mark your business as reopened" and "Mark your business as closed" say a location that was closed by mistake can be reopened from the profile. Sign in with the account that manages it, open Edit profile, and set the business as open with its main hours.

3. If you can't edit it

  • You can't sign in to the profile: claim it from Maps ("Claim this business" or "Manage now") and verify. See our failed verification guide if that stalls.
  • The status reverts: someone may have reported it closed, or there may be a duplicate or an old listing for the same place. Check for duplicates.
  • The profile is suspended: that is a different process. See suspended profiles.

If none of that works, contact Business Profile support through Google's help pages. Google decides, and timing varies.

Mistakes to avoid

  • Creating a new profile because the old one says closed. It can leave you with a duplicate.
  • Leaving seasonal or holiday closures set as permanent. Use special hours or temporary closure instead.
  • Ignoring it. Customers who see "Permanently closed" rarely check further.
Avoid: anyone who guarantees a status change or ranking recovery. Only Google controls what the profile shows.

When to get help

If you can't tell why it's marked closed or can't get access, we can review the profile, explain what to ask Google for and walk you through it. The decisions stay with Google.
