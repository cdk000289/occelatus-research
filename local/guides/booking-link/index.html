Guide · Booking links

Wrong "Book" button on your Google listing? How to fix booking links.

A customer taps "Book online" on your Google profile and lands in a booking app you stopped using, on a page that errors, or on your contact form instead of your calendar. Or you take appointments and there's no Book button at all. Salons, barbers, spas, nail studios, clinics and home-service businesses run into this most. Here's how the links work and what you can change yourself.

Updated November 24, 2026 · Plain-English guide for business owners

Short version: Your Google profile can show two kinds of booking links: ones you added (you can edit or delete them) and ones a booking provider connected through Google (you can hide them with a switch, but only the provider can delete them). Tap your own Book button on a phone, see where it goes, then fix it under Booking in your profile. If a provider won't take its link down, Google gives them 5 business days before you can report it.

How Google booking links work

  • Links you add yourself. You can add up to 10 booking links and mark one as "Business preferred", so it shows first (Google's help page). You can edit or remove these any time.
  • Links from a booking provider. If you use a booking app that works with Google (the "Reserve with Google" program), the provider can put its own booking button on your profile. You can hide it with a switch, but you can't delete it from your profile settings; the provider has to (Google: set up bookings through a provider).
  • Buttons that appear on their own. In November 2026 several local-search consultants noticed "Book online" buttons on profiles that never set one up, some pointing to the business's contact page, some to a Google page that showed an error (Search Engine Roundtable). Google hasn't explained it, so check yours.
  • No Book option at all. Google decides which action buttons a category can have. If you take appointments and see no Booking section, check that your primary category describes an appointment-based service.

The 10-minute check

  1. On your phone, search your business name in Google and in Google Maps.
  2. Tap every action button on your profile: Book, Call, Website, Directions. Note where each one lands.
  3. For the Book button, ask: is it your current booking app? Does it show the right services, staff and prices? Does it load at all?
  4. Open your profile's settings (in Google Search: search your business name, then select Booking; in Maps: Edit profile › Booking). You'll see your own links and any provider links, each with a switch.

Fixing it

  • Your own link is wrong: edit it or delete it in the Booking section, then set the right one as "Business preferred".
  • An old booking app still shows: turn its switch off and select Confirm. Google says existing bookings and reminders stay active, but new bookings through that provider stop. To get the link removed for good, use the provider's opt-out link, support form or account settings. Providers have to process opt-outs within 5 business days; if they don't, you can report it to Google.
  • A Book button you never set up: see where it points. If it goes to your contact page and that's fine with you, leave it, but make sure that page answers fast (people who tap Book expect a time slot, not a form). If you'd rather not have it, add your real booking link as preferred, or contact Google Business Profile support.
  • You want a Book button: add your booking page under Booking › Add link, or connect a provider that works with Google. Some providers charge for bookings made through Google, so check with yours first.
What a Book button won't do. Adding or fixing booking links won't move you up in Google's results; nobody can promise that. It makes sure the customers who already found you can book without hitting a dead end.
