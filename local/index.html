Questions

Straight answers

Do you need my Google password?

No. You add our email as a Manager on your Google Business Profile. That lets us post and reply to reviews, but we never see your password and can't delete your listing. You can remove us anytime with two clicks.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. No card, no sales call. You get a written report by email. If you want us to manage your profile afterward, reply to the email. If not, the report is yours to keep and use.

Do you write fake reviews?

Never. We help you ask real customers for honest reviews and make it easy for them to leave one. Fake reviews break Google's rules, can get your listing suspended, and customers can tell.

Can't I just do this myself?

You can, and the audit will show you how. Most owners don't keep it up because they're busy running the business. We do it every week so you don't have to remember.

Who's behind Occelatus Local?

Occelatus Local is a new service from Occelatus Labs. We're taking on our first 10 clients at the founding price, so each one gets close attention. Questions go straight to local@occelatus.io.

I already have an SEO agency. Do I need this?

We focus only on your Google Business Profile: posts, review replies and new reviews. If your agency already does that, you probably don't need us. If not, we fit alongside them.

My listing is suspended or broken. Can you help?

Yes. Our one-time Profile Fix ($149, credited to your first month if you stay) finds the cause and fixes what can be fixed. Google makes the final call on suspensions, so we'll always tell you honestly what we can and can't do.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month and you can cancel anytime. You keep every review, post and photo, because they live on your own profile.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Local businesses that win customers through Google Maps: plumbers, HVAC, roofers, electricians, auto shops, dentists, med spas, landscapers, restaurants and similar. If people search for you by location, it's for you.
