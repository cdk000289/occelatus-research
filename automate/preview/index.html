Use case — invoice follow-up automation

A service business sends 15–20 invoices a month, with a handful going unpaid past 30 days. We set up automatic reminders that go out on day 3, day 10, and day 20 after the due date. The owner stops manually tracking who owes what — the system flags it, and the reminder goes out on its own.

Before you ask

Questions small business owners
ask us first.

Straight answers. If yours isn't here, ask in the form below and we'll reply within 24 hours.

I'm not technical — can I actually use this?

Yes. That's the entire point of done-for-you. We build, test, and set everything up. You don't install software, write code, or manage anything technical. You get a plain-English explanation of what it does and how to use it — that's it.

What exactly do you mean by "AI-powered automation"?

We use whatever mix of AI tools and automation actually solves your problem — sometimes that's an AI system that reads and responds to messages, sometimes it's simpler rule-based automation moving data between tools. You don't need to know the difference. You tell us the problem; we pick the right tool for it.

How fast will something actually be live?

Most first automations are live within days of your intake form, not weeks. The $99 Blueprint itself turns around in 48 hours.

What does the free assessment include — is there a catch?

No catch. Describe your top 2–3 manual processes and we'll reply with an honest take on whether automation actually helps, roughly what it would look like, and a ballpark cost. If it won't move the needle for your business, we'll tell you that instead of trying to sell you something. No card required.

What if the Blueprint isn't useful to me?

Full money-back guarantee. If it isn't genuinely useful, you don't pay. If you do move forward with a build, the Blueprint fee is credited toward it.

What kinds of things can you actually automate?

If it's repetitive and touches software you already use — email, a CRM, a spreadsheet, a booking calendar, a payment processor — it's usually automatable: replying to leads, chasing invoices, syncing customer data, sending follow-ups, generating reports, and more. Not sure yours qualifies? Describe it in the form below and we'll give you a straight answer.

Am I locked into a monthly contract?

No. Every automation we build is yours to keep, whether or not you continue with us. The $299/mo plan is optional — it covers ongoing changes and new automations — and you can pause or cancel anytime.

What do you need from me to get started?

Just the manual tasks that are eating your time, and the tools you already use. We handle the rest — you review and approve before anything goes live.
