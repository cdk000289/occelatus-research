Automation Blueprint: Ridgeline Home Services (sample)

Prepared by Occelatus · Sample document · Format identical to a real founding-client Blueprint
Every number below is an estimate built from the (fictional) intake answers. Real results depend on volume, data quality and whether the team actually uses the automation. The math is shown on every line so you can check it yourself.

1. Snapshot

  • What they do: Heating, cooling and plumbing repairs for homeowners in one metro area.
  • Team: 6 people: owner, office manager, 4 field technicians. The office manager would own the automations.
  • Current tools: Website contact form, Google Workspace (Gmail, Calendar, Sheets), QuickBooks Online, a field-service app for job scheduling.
  • Biggest pain, in their words: "The office manager spends half the day copying things between apps and chasing people."
  • Hourly value used for estimates: $35/hr (the office manager's loaded cost, as stated in the intake).

2. The three opportunities (ranked)

Ranking = estimated hours saved × confidence ÷ build effort. That's why the smallest-dollar item can rank first: it's quick to build and very likely to work.

Opportunity 1: Send the review request and job summary automatically when a job is closed

  • Today: After each job, the office manager emails the customer a summary and a review link by hand. Roughly 100 jobs a month at about 4 minutes each, and some get forgotten.
  • Automated:
    • Job marked "complete" in the field-service app triggers the workflow
    • Pulls customer name, service performed and technician notes
    • Sends a short thank-you email with the job summary and a review link
    • Logs the send to a Google Sheet; one reminder after 3 days if there's no click
  • Tools involved: field-service app, Gmail, Google Sheets · built in n8n
  • Effort to build: S, about 3–5 hours
  • Estimated time saved: 5.3–6.3 hrs/month
    100 jobs/month × 4 min = 6.7 hrs; 80–95% automated → 5.3–6.3 hrs
  • Estimated value: $187–$222/month at $35/hr (estimate)
  • Risks: technician notes may be too rough to send as-is (fix: send a template summary, not raw notes); field-service API limits.
  • How we'd know it worked: share of completed jobs that get a review request within 1 hour (target: over 95%).

Opportunity 2: Follow up on overdue invoices automatically

  • Today: About 27 invoices a month go past due. The office manager checks QuickBooks, then emails or calls, about 15 minutes per overdue invoice across all follow-ups.
  • Automated:
    • Runs daily and reads unpaid invoices from QuickBooks
    • Sends a polite reminder with the pay link at 3, 10 and 21 days overdue
    • Stops as soon as payment is recorded
    • Posts a weekly list of anything over 30 days so a human can call
  • Tools involved: QuickBooks Online, Gmail · built in n8n
  • Effort to build: S, about 4–6 hours
  • Estimated time saved: 4.7–6.1 hrs/month
    27 overdue invoices × 15 min = 6.75 hrs; 70–90% automated → 4.7–6.1 hrs
  • Estimated value: $165–$213/month at $35/hr (estimate). Faster payment is a possible extra benefit but isn't counted here.
  • Risks: a reminder going out on an invoice that's in dispute (fix: a "do not chase" tag the office manager can set).
  • How we'd know it worked: office-manager hours on collections per month; average days-to-pay before vs after.

Opportunity 3: Reply to every new web lead within 2 minutes

  • Today: About 120 leads a month come in through the contact form and missed calls. The office manager replies when free, often hours later, about 6 minutes each to read, reply and add to the schedule.
  • Automated:
    • New form submission (or missed-call notification) triggers the workflow
    • Sends an immediate reply with a link to pick an available time slot
    • Creates the customer and a tentative job in the field-service app
    • Flags anything that sounds urgent ("leak", "no heat") to the office manager's phone
  • Tools involved: website form, phone system notifications, Google Calendar, field-service app · built in n8n
  • Effort to build: M, about 8–12 hours
  • Estimated time saved: 7.2–9.6 hrs/month
    120 leads/month × 6 min = 12 hrs; 60–80% automated → 7.2–9.6 hrs
  • Estimated value: $252–$336/month at $35/hr (estimate). Any extra jobs won through faster replies aren't counted, because we can't estimate them honestly without data.
  • Risks: missed-call capture depends on the phone provider; booking links don't suit every job type.
  • How we'd know it worked: median time from lead to first reply (today: hours; target: under 2 minutes).
