Questions

Good to know

Do you get access to my inbox?

No. You never give us your password or connect your account. You set up a forwarding rule in your own email so only new inquiry notifications are copied to us, and you can delete that rule anytime. Everything else in your inbox stays private.

Will couples know it was written by AI?

The draft is written by AI from your information. You read it, edit anything you want, and send it yourself from your own email, so you're always the one replying.

Does it work with HoneyBook, Dubsado or The Knot?

It's built to work alongside them, not replace them. If the tool emails you the couple's message, a simple forward rule sends a copy to us; we check this with you on the setup call. You keep using your CRM exactly as you do now.

What if a couple asks something my info doesn't cover?

We flag it and tell you what's missing instead of making something up. Every draft is checked against your information before you see it.

What do you do with my inquiries?

We use them only to draft your replies. We never contact your clients, never sell data, and you can ask us to delete everything at any time.
