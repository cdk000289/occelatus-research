Questions owners ask

Do I need to learn AI or buy new software?

No. We build around the phone, email, calendar and tools you already use. You'll get a short plain-English note on what runs and what to do if you want something changed.

Will this replace my staff?

That's not the goal. It takes the repetitive parts off their plate (texting back, reminding, chasing) so the people you have can spend time on customers.

What if something breaks?

The monthly fee covers watching it and fixing it. If something stops working, we fix it at no extra charge.

Who owns it if I stop?

Your accounts, your customer list and your data stay yours. If you cancel, we hand over what we built and how it works, or switch it off, whichever you prefer.

Are you really local?

Yes. Occel is run from Pensacola by Occelatus Labs LLC, a Florida company. We're happy to meet in person.

Is my customers' information safe?

We only connect what a job needs, never ask for your passwords to be emailed, and don't sell or share your data. Messages to your customers only go to people who contacted you or did business with you.
