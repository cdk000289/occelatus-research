Unlock date: July 15, 2026  ·  T-27  ·  Data: CoinGecko + DexScreener + Tokenomist

Starknet (STRK) is scheduled to unlock 127M tokens — 3.74% of released supply — on July 15, 2026. At $4.34M unlock value against $17.8M daily volume, this is the highest unlock-to-volume ratio of all current active events (0.244x). On-chain liquidity is effectively concentrated in a single Ekubo STRK/USDC pool ($601K), making a $10K exit cost ~3.3% slippage and a $50K exit ~16%. With 96%+ of native DEX liquidity held in one pool and no meaningful routing alternatives on Starknet, exit conditions could shift materially as the date approaches.
