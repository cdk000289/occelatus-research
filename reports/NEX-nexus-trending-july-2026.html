What Is Nexus?

Nexus positions itself as a high-performance Exchange Layer 1 blockchain purpose-built for verifiable finance. The project aims to create a "Decentralized Internet Exchange" powered by zero-knowledge proof technology. The token is deployed on both Ethereum and BNB Chain, giving it broad ecosystem exposure. It recently received a Binance Alpha Spotlight — a curated early-access program for tokens Binance considers high potential — which is widely cited as a near-term catalyst for price action.
